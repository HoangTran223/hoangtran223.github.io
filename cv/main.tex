\section{Research Interests}
My research focuses on the post-training, generalization, and agentic capabilities of large language models (LLMs). I am particularly interested in \textbf{Knowledge Distillation}, \textbf{Self-Play Fine-Tuning}, and \textbf{Deep Research Agents}. I am eager to explore new research directions.
